# SmartEdu Cloud — User Notification Preferences

## 1. Category Toggles
Users can configure in-app notification preferences across academic categories:
- `academic_alerts`: Attendance shortage warnings and prerequisite notifications.
- `assignment_reminders`: Upcoming due dates and new assignment releases.
- `exam_notifications`: Timetable schedules and proctored exam announcements.
- `result_notifications`: Grades and transcript releases.
- `announcements_enabled`: Scoped course, department, and university broadcasts.
- `ai_insights_enabled`: Personalized study recommendations and early risk advice.
- `email_enabled`: University email digest readiness.

## 2. Mandatory Overrides
Institutional emergency notices (`priority = URGENT` or `priority = CRITICAL`) bypass category mutes to ensure critical campus security and emergency notices reach all users.
